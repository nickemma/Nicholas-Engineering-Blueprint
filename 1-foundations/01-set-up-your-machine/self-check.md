### 01 From a new laptop to a running program

1. Why does this course run on Linux, even if your laptop doesn't?
2. On Windows, why should your code live under `~` and not under `/mnt/c/...`?
3. What does a checksum prove about a download, and what doesn't it prove?
4. After installing Go, `go version` said `command not found`. Why, and what fixed it?
5. `uname -m` said `x86_64` but your program said `amd64`. Are they different? How do you know?
